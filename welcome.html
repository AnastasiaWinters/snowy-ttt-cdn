Before you spawn, take a moment to review these rules. It only takes a minute and helps keep every round fair.

Hotkeys and Commands

Note: Available hotkeys and commands may vary with your preferences.

  • i - Open the inventory
  • Q - Drop your weapon
  • C - Open the traitor/detective shop
  • F1 - View the tutorial and custom binds
  • F2 - Mute players while dead
  • F3 - Open this rules menu
  • F4 - Open the event menu
  • F6 - Adjust the HUD
  • F8 - Open the damage logs
  • !motd - Open this rules menu
  • !menu - Open the command menu
  • !block [name] - Block and mute a player
  • !playtime [name] - View a player's total playtime
  • !pm [name] [msg] - Send a private message
  • !trade [name] - Send a trade request
  • !unblock [name] - Unblock and unmute a player

Server Rules

  • RDM and Killing off Suspicion
    • Do not kill players based on suspicion or reasons unrelated to the current round.
  • Ghosting
    • Do not give living players information about the current round after you die.
  • Hateful Conduct
    • Treat everyone respectfully. Hate speech, slurs, and targeted insults are not permitted.
  • OGT - Out of Game Trading
    • Do not exchange in-game items or currency for anything outside the game; both parties may face an inventory wipe or ban.
  • Scamming
    • Scamming is not tolerated, including keeping a temporary trade item, misstating a price, or misleading another player.
  • Exploiting
    • Do not abuse glitches or unintended bugs for personal gain.
  • Bad Usernames
    • Change your name when a staff member asks you to do so.
  • Spamming
    • Do not spam text or voice chat, trade requests, doors, or sounds. Stop when asked.
  • Traitor/Karma Baiting
    • If you are innocent, do not imitate a traitor to make another player lose karma.

Server Clarifications

These notes clarify common questions. Staff may use discretion when applying the guidance below.

  • Association
    • Association includes standing by while a traitor kills, or remaining near a body when a traitor has clearly left an easy target alive.
  • Killing AFK's
    • Do not kill an AFK player before Overtime. Killing an innocent AFK player can still result in punishment.
    • During Overtime, you may kill an AFK player without asking first, though asking whether they have moved is recommended.
    • AFK means the player is not actively playing; standing still briefly does not automatically qualify.
  • Delaying
    • Traitors who unnecessarily delay the round may be warned or slain immediately.
  • Claiming
    • During Overtime, announce a claimed room in chat and give one warning before KOSing or killing anyone who enters.
    • Claimed rooms must be reasonably sized, have only one entrance or exit including traitor doors, and not serve a function such as a tester.
  • KOS - Kill On Sight
    • Kill On Sight is an instruction to kill the named player as soon as you see them.
  • Killing Off of Suspicion
    • Do not kill a player solely because they might be a traitor.
  • The "15 Second Rule"
    • After three chat warnings spaced five seconds apart, you may kill a player who blocks a door or follows you.
    • Include their name in every warning. If they continue after the third, you may KOS or kill them.
  • Traitor Weapons
    • If you obtain a traitor weapon as an innocent, others may kill you. Announce it if you want to avoid suspicion, but do not report the player who owned it.
  • Your Account = Your Responsibility
    • You are responsible for all actions taken on your account.
  • RDM is not allowed. Always have a valid reason before killing.
    • Valid Kills (Allowed Reasons)
      • They shot at you or another player.
      • They killed an innocent; ask them to identify the body.
      • Another player called a KOS on them.
      • They are visibly carrying a traitor weapon.
      • They are the only person in a confined space who could have made the kill or fired the shot.
      • They are destroying innocent or detective equipment.
      • You have DNA proving they are the player responsible, even in a group.
      • They dealt any amount of damage to you; even one point permits a KOS.
    • RDM (Not Allowed)
      • Do not kill someone because they killed you in the previous round.
      • Do not call a KOS based only on a player model.
      • Do not call a KOS based only on a player weapon.
      • Do not call a KOS based only on a player location.
      • Do not call a KOS because someone fired at a wall or tested a weapon.
      • Do not call a KOS because someone is standing in a doorway; follow the 15-second rule.
      • Do not call a KOS because someone is following you; follow the 15-second rule.
      • Ask a player to identify a body before calling a KOS.

        If you did not see the fight begin, warn the victor in text chat to identify the body.
        If you watched the player make the kill and immediately leave without trying to identify the body, you may call a KOS without warning.

        What if someone starts a gunfight without cause?

        You may do so only when:

        • You saw the fight begin, with Person A firing on Person B.
        • No prior KOS was called on Person B, and Person A called no KOS before firing.
        • You saw no traitorous action from Person B that justified the attack.

        In that case:

        You may kill the attacker immediately without warning.

      • Do not call a KOS because someone refuses to test.
      • Do not call a KOS because someone took no damage from a traitor explosive.
  • Ghosting of any kind is prohibited.
    • Ghosting means sharing round information outside the game or through private messages in a way that affects play.
    • Metagaming means using outside knowledge to pursue an objective other than playing the round normally; it is not allowed.
    • Do not help other players complete bounties.
  • Do not exploit.
    • Map exploits, including using invisible textures, are punishable by a slay.
    • Prop surfing is allowed only when it does not reach an area considered an exploit.
    • Do not bypass the AFK system to farm playtime or items.
    • Do not exploit items, inventory, trading, gambling, or related systems.
  • Intentional scamming is prohibited.
    • Examples include keeping a temporary trade item, misstating a price, or deceiving another player. Both parties should understand the real value of every trade.
    • Never trade in-game items for anything outside the game.
    • Keep items and IC on the accounts where they were earned.
  • Hacking and cheating are prohibited.
    • Do not attempt to crash or disrupt the server.
    • Do not spam voice or text chat.
    • Do not harass other players.
    • Do not blackmail anyone over reports, trades, or private information.
  • Trolling is not allowed.
    • Use common sense and do not deliberately disrupt other players.
    • Taunting is allowed when it remains reasonable.
  • Keep your name and profile picture appropriate.
    • Names must include at least three consecutive English letters so they can be pronounced.
    • Choose a name that does not confuse gameplay, such as imitating a KOS call or a role.
  • Hateful conduct includes, but is not limited to:
    • Slurs, including disguised or altered versions used in the same way, are not welcome.
    • Racial slurs, severe sexism, and severe racism are prohibited.
    • Do not direct hateful remarks at a person or group based on identity or beliefs.
    • These rules apply on the server and forums, including attempts to continue the behavior elsewhere.
    • Never encourage anyone to harm themselves.

Traitor Specific

  • Do not reveal the identities of your fellow traitors.
  • Do not kill your fellow traitors.
  • Do not deliberately let innocents enter traitor rooms.
  • Do not unnecessarily delay the round; actively pursue the innocent team.
  • Do not cooperate with innocents or detectives.

Detective Specific

  • Disarming players without a reasonable suspicion is trolling.
  • Do not KOS a player solely for refusing to test.

Notes

Trying to evade a punishment may lead to a more severe punishment.
