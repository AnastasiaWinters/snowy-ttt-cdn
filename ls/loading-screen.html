How to play

Innocent

  • Find and eliminate all Traitors while keeping as many allies alive as possible.
  • Gain the trust of a Detective to find a reliable ally.

Detective

  • Find and eliminate all Traitors using your specialized shop and gadgets designed to counter Traitors.
  • Find friendly Innocents who can cover your back.

Traitor

  • Eliminate all other players except your allies and Jesters while staying undetected.
  • Use credits to acquire powerful force multipliers, such as an AWP or an inconspicuous turtle mine.

Jester

  • Bait other players into killing you to win.

Controls

  • I Open inventory
  • C Open Detective or Traitor shop
  • Q Drop weapon
  • E Interact and pick up or swap weapons
  • F1 View tutorial and other settings
  • F6 Adjust HUD
